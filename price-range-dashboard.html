CSV 字段说明 & FactSet 取数提示 & 方法说明File formats, FactSet field hints & methodology notes

companies.csv (one row per company): ticker, name, currency, price, price_date, eps_fy1_low/_mean/_high, eps_fy2_low/_mean/_high. FactSet: FG_PRICE, FE_ESTIMATE("EPS","LOW|MEAN|HIGH",…).

history.csv (one row per month, 5–10y recommended): ticker, date, pe_ntm (or price + eps_ntm; a price column also feeds the volatility band). FactSet: FG_PE_NTM.

FactSet model .xlsx ("Earnings Per Share" block): reads FY1/FY2 annual EPS means; derives price from the Valuation block's FY1 forward PE.

Estimate History .xlsx: reads latest consensus EPS low/mean/high and the monthly P/E series. It tracks a fixed fiscal year, so only the most recent 24 months are used for percentiles — for a true 5–10y valuation band export an NTM PE monthly series. The full price series (P/E × mean) still feeds the volatility band.

Snapshot .xlsx ("Previous Close"): real closing price, 52-week range, analyst target/rating, annual PE references. Price precedence: manual > snapshot/CSV > model-derived.

Other methods not computed here: DCF + sensitivity (needs cash-flow assumptions; unstable when near-term FCF is depressed by heavy capex), option-implied volatility bands (needs option IV data), technical support/resistance (needs daily price history). The statistical volatility band in the comparison panel uses realized monthly volatility: price × e±σ over one year, a 68% band under a lognormal assumption.

FactSet 公司模型 .xlsx(含 "Earnings Per Share" 区块):自动读取 FY1/FY2 年度 EPS 预测均值,并用 Valuation 区块的 FY1 PE 反推现价。

FactSet Estimate History .xlsx(含 "Estimate History" 表格):自动读取最新一致预期的 EPS 低/均/高,以及月度 P/E 序列并计算分位。注意:Estimate History 跟踪的是固定财年,窗口拉太长时早期 P/E 是"当时股价 ÷ 多年后盈利",口径与近期不可比,因此 app 最多采用最近 24 个月;真正的 5–10 年估值带请导出 NTM PE 月度序列(history.csv 格式)。

FactSet Snapshot .xlsx(含 "Previous Close" 的快照页):自动读取真实收盘价、52 周区间、分析师目标价/评级和年度 PE 参考。现价来源优先级:手动输入 > 快照/CSV 实价 > 模型反推。所有文件可以同时拖入,互相补全同一家公司。首次解析 xlsx 需联网加载解析库。

本工具未计算的其他测算方法:DCF + 敏感性矩阵(需现金流假设;近期 FCF 被大额资本开支压低的公司结果极不稳定)、期权隐含波动率区间(需期权 IV 数据)、技术面支撑/阻力(需日线价格)。方法对比面板里的"波动率统计区间"用已实现月度波动率计算:现价 × e±σ(一年期),对数正态假设下约 68% 概率带。history.csv 若含 price 列,也会用于波动率计算。

方法说明:隐含价 = EPS 情景 × 历史 NTM PE 分位。区间反映"盈利预测不确定性 × 估值波动范围"的组合,不构成投资建议;估值分位基于导入的历史窗口,窗口选择本身即是假设。Method: implied price = EPS scenario × historical PE percentile. Ranges combine earnings uncertainty with valuation volatility; not investment advice. Percentiles depend on the imported history window — the window itself is an assumption.
